Description

One sentence: Ink Well Keeper is a free iPhone and iPad app that scans Disney Lorcana cards with the camera, tracks set completion and live collection value, and builds format-legal decks you can share as a link.

Short: Ink Well Keeper turns a shoebox of Lorcana cards into an organized, valued collection in minutes. Point the camera at a card — or a whole stack — and it's recognized and logged automatically, with live market pricing, set-completion tracking across every set and variant (foils, Enchanteds, promos), and a deck builder that validates Core, Infinity, and Casual format legality. Decks share as QR codes or links that open in any browser. Built by a solo developer, free with no ads, and all collection data stays on the user's device.

Story angles

A solo developer built the card scanner Lorcana collectors were waiting for

Lorcana's collector base exploded faster than its tooling. While most players tracked collections in spreadsheets or web checklists, one indie developer shipped a native iOS scanner that logs a whole stack of cards through the camera in one session — then keeps score of what each pull is worth with live market pricing.

The set-release-day app: new cards supported the morning a set drops

Every Lorcana set release sends collectors scrambling to log pulls. Ink Well Keeper ships day-one support for each new set — all 242 Attack of the Vine cards were scannable at launch — making it a natural companion for box openings, pre-release events, and set-drop content.

AI in a fan-made TCG app that stays free where it counts

An AI deck builder that completes decks from cards you actually own, and a rules assistant for "can I do X?" table disputes — while every core collecting feature stays free with no ads and data stays on-device. A counterpoint to subscription-first collector apps.

Key features

  • Batch camera scanning — scan a whole stack of cards in one session; a full box logged in minutes
  • Set completion — visual completion bars and missing-card filters for all 13 sets plus promos, with foil, Enchanted, Epic, and Iconic variants tracked separately
  • Live pricing — per-card market prices and total collection value, updated automatically
  • Deck builder — Core / Infinity / Casual format legality validation, mana curve, missing-cards view, and QR/link deck sharing that opens in any browser
  • Import — Dreamborn CSV and plain card-list import, plus import from the official app's backup
  • AI extras (Pro) — AI Deck Builder that completes a deck from your collection, and a Rules Assistant for rules questions
  • Private by design — no account required; data on-device with optional iCloud sync

From the developer

I built Ink Well Keeper because logging a booster box by hand was taking longer than opening it. The goal has always been simple: point your camera at your cards and let the app do the bookkeeping — for free, without ads, and without your collection leaving your device. — Brevin Blalock, developer
Everything Lorcana-related in the app is free and always will be. Pro exists to pay for the two features that cost me money to run — the AI deck builder and the rules assistant — nothing else sits behind it. — Brevin Blalock, developer
