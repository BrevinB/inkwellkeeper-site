Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Scanning, tracking, prices, sets, decks and binders are all free. Ink Well Keeper Pro adds the AI deck builder and rules assistant.

Can I bring my collection over from iPhone or another app?

Yes. Import from the official Disney Lorcana app, a Dreamborn or Collectr CSV, or an Ink Well Keeper export from your iPhone. iCloud sync doesn't carry over to Android, so use export and import.

What do testers need to do?

Use the app like you normally would and keep it installed for two weeks. If something breaks or confuses you, email support@inkwellkeeper.app. I read everything.

When does it launch for everyone?

As soon as the 14-day test wraps and Google approves it. Testers keep the app and get updates automatically.

Which phones does it support?

Android 10 and newer.
